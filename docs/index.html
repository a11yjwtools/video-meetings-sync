      How to use

      1. Put on headphones. If they are Bluetooth, choose Bluetooth in Settings once.
      2. Tap Start listening and allow the microphone the first time.
      3. Hold the phone normally. When a video plays in the room, the app finds it within a few seconds and plays its audio description in your headphones, in sync.
      4. Once it is in sync, the microphone switches off and the description plays to the end. Then the app listens for the next video.
      5. If the description ever sounds out of step, tap Find my place again.

      You can also use the play and pause controls on your headphones or lock screen: pause stops the description, play finds your place again.

      Descriptions are streamed from jw.org, so you need an internet connection.
